# Brainstorming Session Results

**Facilitator:** Sammorgan
**Date:** 2026-02-07

## Session Overview

**Topic:** Co-op Backpack Hero with Traitor Mechanics
**Goals:** Define the core Gameplay Loop, Traitor Mechanics, and Win/Loss conditions
**Outcome:** Complete redesign of the game loop centered on "Drafting", "Storytelling", and a "Real-Time Mega-Bag Finale".

### Session Setup

We are exploring a pivot to a "Backpack Hero" style inventory management game with a heavy emphasis on **coasting co-op mechanics** (hitchhiking/collaborative travel) and **social deduction** (Among Us style traitor).

Key Focus Areas:
1.  **Start**: How the team forms and gears up.
2.  **Day Cycle**: The "Backpack" phase (Synergy) vs. "Traitor" phase (Sabotage).
3.  **End**: How the game culminates (Destination vs. Betrayal).

## Technique Execution: What If Scenarios

**Phase 1: Expansive Exploration**
**Focus:** Generate wild ideas about Traitor mechanics and Co-op synergy without constraints.

**Ideas Generated:**

**[End Game]**: The Voltron Backpack
*Concept:* At the final boss, all players' backpacks merge into one massive grid.
*Novelty:* Players must plan their individual layouts to fit into the hypothetical "Mega-Bag" at the end. Radical co-op finale.

**[Traitor]**: The Schrödinger's Traitor
*Concept:* There is a 0-1 chance of a traitor. The team never knows for sure if *anyone* is sabotageing them.
*Novelty:* Pure paranoia. Mistakes might just be mistakes, or active malice.

**[Sabotage]**: Midnight Swap
*Concept:* Traitors can swap one item between two other players' bags during the night phase.
*Novelty:* Frames other players. "I didn't pack that!" "Yes you did, I saw it!"

**[Progression]**: Dynamic Capacity
*Concept:* Narrative events permanently alter bag size (e.g., "Tore a hole" = -2 slots, "Found a pouch" = +2 slots).
*Novelty:* Physical representation of the journey's wear and tear.

**[Mechanics]**: Cursed Synergies
*Concept:* Items that are powerful but sabotage adjacent items (e.g., "Radioactive Core" powers weapons but rots food).
*Novelty:* Risk/Reward management that affects neighbors.

**[Mechanics]**: Obstacle Synergy
*Concept:* Synergies aren't just for combat numbers, but for overcoming environmental puzzles (e.g., Rope + Hook = Climbing Gear).
*Novelty:* Utility-based synergy rather than just stat-sticking.

**[Itemization]**: The Keyholder
**[End Game Refined]**: The Real-Time Mega-Bag
*Concept:* All grids merge. *Everyone* acts at once (real-time mouse cursors).
*Twist:* The Traitor can *also* move items.
*Conflict:* Is that person organizing for synergy, or ruining it? "Stop moving the Sword!"

**[Traitor Refined]**: Schrödinger's Sabotage
*Concept:* The game might not even *have* a traitor, but "glitches" (items un-equipping, swapping) happen randomly to simulate one.

## Technique Execution: Attribute Listing

**Phase 2: Pattern Recognition**
**Focus:** Break down the successful ideas into components to find the fun.

**Target 1: The Mega-Bag (End Game)**
*   **Size**: Massive Grid (All players combined).
*   **Constraint**: **Real-Time Timer**. "Mad Rush".
*   **Visibility**: **Full Info on Hover**. (Essential for coordination).
*   **Mechanic**: Simultaneous dragging.

**Target 2: Traitor Actions (The "Sabotage")**
*   **The Problem**: How to distinguish "Maice" from "Incompetence"?
*   **Attribute - Cooldown**: Does everyone have a drag cooldown?
*   **Attribute - Locking**: Can players "Lock" an item? Can Traitors break locks?
*   **Refined Attributes:**
*   **No Trash**: Items cannot be removed, only re-arranged.
*   **Item-Based Locking**: Specific items (e.g., "Anchor", "Padlock") lock themselves or neighbors in place.
*   **The Conflict**: Traitor tries to move items to break adjacency bonuses; Team tries to lock them in perfect spots.

## Technique Execution: Storyboarding

**Phase 4: Action Planning**
**Focus:** Map out the complete Game Loop.

**The New Cycle:**

1.  **Lobby**:
    *   Players join.
    *   **Role Assignment**: Game rolls for Traitor (0 or 1). *Nobody knows if there is one.*

2.  **Morning (The Draft)**:
    *   A spread of items appears (based on Day difficulty).
    *   Players take turns picking 1 item at a time until bags are full or pool is empty.

3.  **Journey (The Split)**:
    *   **Fork in the Road**: The team *must* split resources.
        *   *Example*: "The Cave requires 2 people. The Ridge requires 1."
    *   **Solo Encounters**: No witnesses. Perfect for Traitor bluffs.
    *   **Duo Encounters**: You watch your partner fight. Harder to lie.
    *   **Tactical Loss**:
        *   **Win**: Get Standard Loot (+Stats).
        *   **Lose**: Take Damage (-Morale) but get **Cursed Scrap**.
        *   *Risk*: 3x Cursed Scrap combines into a **God-Tier Item** for the Boss.
        *   *Bluff*: "I didn't lose on purpose, the Wolf was strong!" (Actually farming Scrap).

4.  **Campfire (The Debrief)**:
    *   Players "Tell their stories".
    *   "I fought a Bear and found this Scrap." -> Truth or Lie?
    *   Vote to heal or track suspicion.

5.  **Finale (Day 5 - The Mega-Bag)**:
    *   **Boss Fight**.
    *   All bags merge. Real-time chaos.
    *   Use items (and God-Tier Scrap weapons) to defeat Boss HP.
